# Sound sources

Every file the pack's sounds are mixed from (by `sounds.sh`). All are public domain (CC0): no
attribution is required, but here they are.

| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| spaceEngineLarge_000, forceField_003, lowFrequency_explosion_000, explosionCrunch_000/004 | [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 |
| impactMetal_heavy_001 | [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 |
| oga-rubberduck-cannon_02 | [25 CC0 bang / firework SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx) | rubberduck | CC0 |
| oga-aquinn-sirens | [Sirens and Alarm Noise](https://opengameart.org/content/sirens-and-alarm-noise) | aquinn | CC0 |
| freesound-506313-daleonfire-falling-bomb | [Falling Bomb.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Daleonfire/sounds/506313/) (HQ preview) | Daleonfire | CC0 |

What each pack sound is made of:

- `strike/charge`: Kenney spaceEngineLarge_000 swelling up over 2.4 s, forceField_003 on top at the end.
- `strike/siren`: the slow air-raid wail from aquinn's sirens (26.2-28.8 s).
- `strike/impact`: Kenney lowFrequency_explosion_000 + rubberduck's cannon_02 + Kenney explosionCrunch_004.
- `drop/incoming`: Daleonfire's falling bomb whistle.
- `drop/land`: Kenney impactMetal_heavy_001 + explosionCrunch_000.
